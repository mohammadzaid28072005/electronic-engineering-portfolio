P / 03Biomedical DSP

ECG signal analysis pipeline

Developed a MATLAB workflow as part of group coursework to compare resting and exercising ECG recordings and recover meaningful heart-rate information from noisy signals.

  • Processed 60-second OpenBCI Ganglion recordings sampled at 200 Hz
  • Removed baseline wander, filtered noise and detected Q, R and S features
  • Compared relaxed and post-exercise recordings: approximately 72 BPM versus 102 BPM by manual count
MATLABButterworthQRS detectionECG

Digital filter design & audio analysis

Designed and evaluated IIR and FIR filters for sampled signals, connecting frequency-domain theory to practical audio processing.

  • Impulse-invariant low-pass, band-stop and band-pass IIR filters
  • Frequency-sampling FIR design with Hamming and Hann windows
  • 512-point FFT analysis of audio and sinusoid identification
MATLABFFTIIR / FIRAudio DSP

100 kW online UPS conceptual design

Developed and simulated a transformerless double-conversion UPS architecture for a data-centre application.

  • Modelled a 400 V three-phase rectifier, 480 V battery-supported DC link and SPWM inverter in Simulink
